## Reusable capabilities added

- `float_valve` / `tank_float_control`: a complete connected tank; a simplified cutaway; a partly submerged ball, constant-length rigid lever and fixed pivot; a guided inlet stem, washer and open seat; rising level throttling the inlet; held closed level; separate draw-off valve and external discharge; falling level reopening the inlet; refill and held final state. Water markers and elapsed time are explanatory, not a CFD prediction. No electronic sensor, toilet flush, steam trap or powered pump is claimed.
- `combination_lock` / `gated_padlock`: a complete three-wheel padlock, simplified housing cutaway, actual rotating gate geometry, one guided locking bar spanning all wheels, a visible short-leg notch and moving shackle. One or two aligned wheels cannot release the bar; the third must provide clearance. The bar then rests in the gates before the shackle is pulled. This is a representative mechanism, not a commercial replica, bypass method or reset procedure.

Each has its own connected object, moving geometry, camera sequence, materials and visual setup. Neither reuses the camera, bridge, generator or basic diagram scene family. Permanent novelty history is unchanged. Selection remains automatic; no authored production topic or dialogue is supplied.

## Stronger generation and release logic

Shared causal writing policy v2 requires a useful concrete opening question and connected explanations rather than a catalogue of parts. New daily plans additionally check named visible parts, static versus moving beats, and absent components. Bounded model repair changes only rejected sections, retains valid peers, and ignores unrequested replacements. Timing cues select unique phrases already present in the model's narration; they never supply new spoken words. The selected visual component narrows the writer's factual context before narration is generated.

The renderer produces a second independent causal report from its actual state under the measured speech timeline. It checks required movement direction, held states, rigid linkage, closed valve flow, three-gate interlock and release-before-pull. Separate counterfactual pixel tests establish that the named internal parts are visible. The delivery seal requires complete reports tied to the exact plan and timeline; empty success labels, reversed measurements, stale evidence or missing channels do not pass.

Daily shorts retain the approved `am_fenrir` narrator, 40–70-second encoded duration, silent camera movement, no default voice subtitles, encoded-audio measurements and manual Studio approval. Existing videos are never regenerated. A failed pre-media project may continue its original reservation; once encoded, its family is consumed even if inspection fails.
