## Factual scope

This is representative iris mechanics, not an exact commercial lens model. It does not simulate focusing, refraction, image inversion, optical blur, a shutter mechanism, or a motor. Light markers and sensor brightness are explanatory illustrations, not a ray-traced photograph. Never claim that aperture alone controls sharpness or that opening it doubles brightness.
